## The paper shown in the screenshots

The screenshots in `docs/screenshots/` show text and figures from:

> Gonçalves, I. G., Wellmann, F. (2025). Uncertainty Propagation in Deep Gaussian Process Networks. *Mathematical Geosciences* 57, 1115–1133. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11004-025-10187-4

published open access under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). **Changes made:** the article was converted into a PaperWeave Work from its LaTeX source; its text was split into Nodes and rendered by the reading app and the PaperWeave LaTeX class, and short summaries at two lengths (Gists and Summaries), primers and typed links between Nodes were added. Those additions are not part of the published article.
